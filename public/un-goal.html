UN Sustainable Development Goal 12

Project Draytonii is built around UN Sustainable Development Goal 12: Responsible Consumption and Production - the goal that asks us to use the planet's resources more efficiently and to make the true cost of what we consume visible.

What Goal 12 Is

Adopted by all UN member states as part of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, Goal 12 calls for doing more and better with less: decoupling economic growth from environmental degradation through sustainable management of natural resources, reduced waste, and responsible consumption patterns. Two of its targets are especially relevant to this project:

  • Target 12.2 - achieve the sustainable management and efficient use of natural resources.
  • Target 12.8 - ensure that people everywhere have the relevant information and awareness for sustainable development and lifestyles in harmony with nature.

How Project Draytonii Supports Goal 12

AI usage is a form of consumption like any other - it draws on electricity, water, and mined minerals - but unlike a grocery receipt or a utility bill, that cost is invisible to the person actually generating it. Project Draytonii exists to close that information gap, which is exactly what Target 12.8 asks for.

  • Turning invisible consumption into a number you can see. Every prompt you send to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini draws real energy, water, and mineral resources. The Dashboard's CO2, electricity, water, and minerals panels put a figure on that consumption instead of leaving it hidden inside someone else's data center.
  • Making the numbers relatable, not just technical. The Comparison tab on each metric converts raw figures - kilograms of CO2eq, kilowatt-hours, liters, kilograms of Sb-eq - into everyday equivalents, so responsible consumption isn't gatekept behind unit conversions only a scientist would recognize.
  • Sourcing every figure transparently. Every estimate on this site traces back to a cited, published source (see the Sources page), consistent with Goal 12's emphasis on credible sustainability reporting rather than unverified claims.
  • Respecting resources in how the tool itself works. Your conversation data is parsed in your browser and never uploaded in raw form (see the Your Data page) - efficient, minimal-footprint handling of your own data, not just the numbers it reports on.

Why It Matters at the AI Scale

No single prompt's footprint is large. But billions of prompts are sent across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini every day, and Goal 12 is built on the premise that individually small, repeated choices are exactly where sustainable consumption is won or lost at scale. Giving people visibility into their own AI usage - and a frame of reference for what that usage actually costs - is a small, concrete step toward the kind of resource-conscious consumption Goal 12 calls for.
