Your Data

This page explains exactly what's inside each platform's official export file, how to request that export yourself, and precisely how this app handles your data once you upload it.

ChatGPT

What's in the export

  • conversations.json - a tree ("mapping") of every message node in every conversation, including your prompts and ChatGPT's replies, per-message timestamps, and, when available, which model answered (e.g. gpt-4o, gpt-4o-mini).
  • chat.html - a browsable HTML copy of your conversations. Not used by this app.
  • user.json, message_feedback.json, and other account metadata files. Not used by this app.

How to get it

  1. Go to chatgpt.com and log in.
  2. Click your profile icon → Settings → Data Controls.
  3. Under "Export data," click Export and confirm.
  4. OpenAI emails you a download link, usually within a few minutes to a few hours. Download the zip from that email.

Claude

What's in the export

  • conversations.json - a flat, already-ordered list of every message in every conversation ("chat_messages"), each tagged human or assistant, with timestamps. Claude's export does not include which model answered each message.
  • users.json and other account metadata. Not used by this app.

How to get it

  1. Go to claude.ai and log in.
  2. Click your profile → Settings → Account.
  3. Find Export data and request it.
  4. Anthropic emails you a download link. Download the zip from that email.

Gemini

What's in the export

Google Takeout doesn't export full Gemini conversations - only an activity log.

  • MyActivity.json - one entry per prompt you sent, with the prompt's title/text and a timestamp. It does not include Gemini's replies or which model answered - this app estimates those separately and clearly flags them as estimated.

How to get it

  1. Go to takeout.google.com and log in.
  2. Click Deselect all, then scroll to find and check only My Activity.
  3. Click into "All activity data included" and deselect everything except Gemini Apps.
  4. Set the export format to JSON (not HTML), then start the export.
  5. Google emails you a download link. Download the zip from that email.

How Your Data Is Used

  • Everything happens in your browser. When you upload an export zip, it's unzipped and parsed entirely client-side in JavaScript - the raw file itself never leaves your device.
  • Only small, derived numbers are sent to our server: which platform, a timestamp, and token-count estimates for each message. The actual text of your prompts and replies is discarded immediately after those counts are computed, and is never transmitted or stored anywhere.
  • Aggregated numbers are stored in a database so your dashboard persists across visits, and are used only to power the charts and stats you see there.
  • You can delete everything at any time using the "Clear All Data" button at the bottom of the Dashboard. This is permanent and cannot be undone.
